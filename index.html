01 // ABOUT ME!

Behind the Screen

Why Cybersecurity?

Every great puzzle starts with an impossible ending. For me, that realization began with magic and sleight of hand. Growing up, I loved card magic and puzzles, and I learned a secret from them: if a card ends up somewhere it has no business being, it was always there. That wasn't just the end of the act, but where the routine actually started from.

True reverse engineering isn’t guessing; it’s standing at the final endpoint and walking backward to find the origin. That compulsion to trace a footprint from end to start naturally shaped my path, leading me to a Bachelor’s in Forensics, where digital evidence completely captured my mind, and later to a Master’s in Cybersecurity.

Today, I look at digital threats through that exact lens. Whether I’m analyzing an anomalous packet or a compromised host, I treat the final artifact like that misplaced card, tracing the path backward until the root vector is laid bare. Driven by a fierce curiosity, I see security not as a compliance checklist, but as the ultimate, unfolding puzzle.

What I'm Focusing On Right Now

Most days, you’ll find me tinkering in local homelabs or working through hands-on investigative scenarios.

Whether I'm digging through log files, breaking down a strange packet, or testing security workflows, it’s less about chasing metrics and more about that quiet satisfaction of following a trail until it makes sense. I just genuinely enjoy figuring out how things work.

Beyond the Terminal

Balance is everything. Whether I’m working out, experimenting in the kitchen, meditating to clear the noise, or getting lost in a good book, stepping away from the screen keeps me grounded. I’ve found that the best mental clarity often happens when you give yourself space to breathe, keeping your perspective sharp for whatever puzzle comes next.
